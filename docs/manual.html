Welcome

How the system fits together

CoreLink is one connected platform with three apps and a set of guest-facing screens. Everything updates in real time — a change made on Control appears on the TV screens and in Admin within a second.

Control
Operators run each attraction — queue times, dispatching groups, status.
Sign-Off
Supervisors complete opening & closing safety checks on iPads.
Admin
Managers oversee the whole park, screens, reports & incidents.

Getting in

  • Open the site and you'll land on the sign-in page. Enter your email and password.
  • You arrive at the landing page — tap the app you need (Control, Sign-Off, or Admin).
  • Switch apps anytime using the logo menu in the top-left of any app.

The Control App

Running an attraction

Control is where operators manage a single attraction on a phone or tablet. You must log in as the operator before you can dispatch or change the queue.

1 · Take the panel

  • Select your attraction from the tabs at the top.
  • The panel is locked until an operator signs in. Tap "Enter PIN to operate".
  • Type your 4-digit PIN. Your name now shows in the green operator chip.
  • To hand over, tap the chip → Change operator. To finish, tap End shift.
Why it matters: every dispatch and queue change is recorded against whoever is logged in, so the park always knows who was operating.

2 · Dispatch groups

Set the group size with − / + (or tap the number to type it), then tap Dispatch group each time you send guests in. The timer shows how long since the last dispatch and turns amber → red if you're running slow.

3 · Queue time & status

Use the −10 / −5 / +5 / +10 buttons to set the wait shown on the entrance screen. Set the status pill to Delayed or Closed when needed — dispatching locks while delayed/closed.

If a delay is logged, a banner appears asking for an incident report. Tap Report to fill it in, or No incident — minor (then confirm) if nothing happened. See the Incidents section.

The Control App

Throughput, reports & alerts

Hourly throughput

The throughput list fills automatically from your dispatches. To correct a figure, tap the small pencil on a row (or press & hold the row) and type the real number.

Show report

At the end of the night tap Show Report under the logo to record operational, technical, construction and costume notes. It's submitted with a PIN.

Alerts from management

Coloured banners appear at the top of the screen when Admin sends an alert — info warning urgent. Tap × to dismiss once read.

The Sign-Off App

Opening & closing safety checks

Sign-Off runs on iPads. Supervisors work through checklists for each attraction and sign them off by role with a PIN — building a full audit trail and the nightly report.

Step by step

  • Tap your attraction tile on the home screen.
  • Open a section (e.g. Technical) and flip each check's green switch as you complete it.
  • When all items are on, tap Sign off section · enter PIN.
  • Completed sections turn into a green receipt showing who signed and when.
  • Repeat for all sections; closing checks unlock for the end of the night.
Only the right role can sign a section. Your PIN carries your roles — if a section needs a role you don't hold, ask the right person to sign it.

Screens & TV displays

Setting up the guest screens

Guest-facing screens run on small Raspberry Pi computers plugged into TVs. They pair to the system with a short code — no keyboard needed at the screen.

Pairing a new screen

  • The Pi boots to a setup screen showing a 4-character code.
  • In Admin → Screens, find the screen and choose what it should display from the dropdown.
  • The screen switches to its assigned view within seconds. Give it a name so it's easy to find later.

Incidents

Reporting & reviewing incidents

Any issue — a guest problem, downtime, or an injury — is logged as an incident. Everything flows into one review queue in Admin, and every report can be printed for the accident book.

Four ways a report starts

On Control

When a delay happens (or Admin asks), a banner appears. Choose Report to file it, or No incident — minor (with a confirm) if there's nothing to record. Operators can also tap Log incident anytime.

The form branches

Guest issue / downtime → type, location, what happened, witness.
Injury or accident → a full HSE report: injured person & contact details, nature of injury, first aid, hospital, witnesses.

RIDDOR is decided by a manager, not the operator. Injuries arrive flagged "RIDDOR: not assessed" — a manager sets the determination and adds the action taken & remediation during review.
